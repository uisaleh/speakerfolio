FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What happens before a training session?

We start with a 30-minute discovery call, then short interviews or a survey with a few of your managers. I use what I learn to tailor the examples, exercises and tools to your team and the change you are facing.

Do you run in-person and virtual sessions?

Yes. Keynotes and workshops run in person anywhere in the UK and Europe, and virtually worldwide. Virtual sessions are designed for cameras on, with breakouts and live polls.

How many people can join a workshop?

Workshops work best with 12 to 30 managers so everyone can practise. Keynotes suit audiences of any size, from a leadership team of 20 to a conference of 1,000.

How do you measure the impact of training?

Every program includes a pre-session survey, a 30-day follow-up survey and a short impact report showing which tools managers are using and what changed for their teams.

How far in advance should we book?

Most clients book six to ten weeks ahead. For multi-session programs or busy months like January and September, booking three months ahead is safest.
