About Marco

I help churches, schools and community groups turn faith into action, heal from hard seasons and build families that last.

Marco grew up in foster care, found faith at nineteen and has spent fifteen years leading youth ministry, reentry programs and family outreach. Today he speaks to congregations, conferences and schools across the country, with stories that are honest, hopeful and practical.

Ask a question
How do we invite Marco to speak?

Send the form below or email the team with your date, location, audience and the kind of event. You'll hear back within two working days with availability and next steps.

Do you accept a love offering?

Yes. For Sunday services at smaller churches, Marco is happy to receive a love offering. Conferences, schools and larger events have a set honorarium, and nonprofits can ask about reduced rates.

How far ahead should we book?

Three to six months ahead is best, especially for spring and fall weekends. Last-minute dates are sometimes available, so it's always worth asking.

Does Marco travel for events?

Yes. Marco is based in San Antonio and travels nationwide. Events within Texas usually only need mileage; flights and one night's hotel are covered for other states.

Can Marco speak online?

Yes. He speaks at virtual conferences, online services and webinars, with a tested camera and sound setup.

Is Marco's message suitable for public schools?

Yes. Youth Rising has a school-safe version that focuses on identity, choices and resilience without religious content, alongside a faith-based version for churches and camps.

Can the message fit our series or theme?

Absolutely. Marco has a planning call with every host to shape his message around your series, theme or season.

Tell us about your community, your date and what you're hoping for. Marco's team replies within two working days.
