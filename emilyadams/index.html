About Emily

From the plant floor to the boardroom.

Emily Adams has spent 15 years building the operational and workforce infrastructure that makes technology, growth and culture actually work. She started in automotive manufacturing at Valeo, led workforce strategy for a 2,500-person operation at Honda Manufacturing of Indiana, built finance operations at ConnectWise that saved more than $6M, and founded Leading With Human Design to help companies simplify operations and develop effective leaders. Today she is Managing Partner at HyLo Inno, Operating Partner at Gulf Works Lab and Chief Operating Officer of Tampa Bay Tech Week, and works with growth-stage companies as a Fractional COO.
