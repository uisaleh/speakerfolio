Meet Delane

Poetry that helps people feel seen, understood and reminded they matter.

Delane writes and performs about the experiences that shape us. Her goal is simple: use her voice and her writing to help people embrace their stories, recognize their worth and find meaning in what they have lived through.

About Delane

She turns vulnerability into encouragement.

Delane is a poet, author and mental-health advocate from Moss Point, Mississippi. Her work centers on mental health, faith, identity, healing and self-worth, and she shares it through spoken-word performances and digital platforms, where she has built an online community of more than 20,000 followers. She has published three poetry books: You Don't Have to Die to Matter, The Kitchen Doesn't Stay Clean for Long and The Lane's Heart Vol. 1. Alongside her writing she has worked in classrooms and behavioral support settings, and she studied Child and Family Sciences at The University of Southern Mississippi.
