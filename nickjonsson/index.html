About

Helping leaders move from isolation to authentic connection.

Nick Jonsson is a TEDx speaker, executive coach, counsellor and psychotherapist based in Singapore, and the international bestselling author of Executive Loneliness. Ranked #28 among the Global Gurus Top 30 Coaching Professionals in 2026, he works with executives, founders and leadership teams around the world.

After more than two decades in senior international leadership, Nick looked successful on the outside while privately struggling with burnout, addiction and loneliness. Rebuilding his life became the foundation of his work. Today he speaks on executive loneliness, psychological safety, holistic leadership and resilience, and he is the creator of the 5 Steps to Holistic Leadership. Away from the stage, he is a top 2% IRONMAN All-World Athlete.

5 Steps to Holistic Leadership™

Perform at your best without burning out.

The framework Nick created from his own recovery and two decades of leading teams.

S

Surrender

Stop carrying it all alone. Admitting you are struggling is where change starts.

P

Purpose

Know why you do what you do, so success means more than the next target.

C

Connection

Build honest relationships and peer support, the opposite of isolation at the top.

G

Goals

Set clear goals for your career, your health and your life outside work.

D

Discipline

How do you eat an elephant? One bite at a time: small habits, done every day.
