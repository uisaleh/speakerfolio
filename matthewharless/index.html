A working registered nurse for nearly twenty years, Matthew names what broken systems do to the people inside healthcare, and helps them get their hope back.

He has cared for patients in psychiatric units, emergency departments, med-surg floors and hospital resource teams across Indiana. He is the author of How Healthcare Dies and Transition to Care, host of The Moral Injury Hour, co-founder of The Business of Hope with Ashley Chancellor and creator of The Harless Method. His goal is simple: no nurse should believe they are the problem when the system is what’s broken.
