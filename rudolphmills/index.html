About

The thing that almost broke you can build what you were called to become.

Rudolph Valentino Mills III is the founder of Push to Build, a purpose-first consulting practice built on biblical principles. He is Fatherhood Program Manager at Healthy Baby Network in Rochester, NY, founder and leader of Worship Experience Ministries, and CEO of the WE C.A.R.E. Family Initiative.

He is the author of The Preacher’s Corner, Volume One, and co-author of Same Destination, Different Journey. A retired Christian clean comedian and father of seven, he speaks on turning pain into purpose through the H.U.R.T. Framework, helping people use their hardships and past experiences as fuel instead of reasons to stay stuck.

The H.U.R.T. Framework

Four steps from pain to purpose.

The framework at the heart of every Push to Build keynote, workshop and coaching session.

H

Hurt Happens

Pain is part of every story. Naming it honestly is the first step to moving through it.

U

Understand the Process

Seeing what the season is teaching you, instead of only what it is taking from you.

R

Reframe the Pain

Turning excuses, hardships and past experiences into motivation and tools.

T

Trust the Purpose

Pushing forward with faith that what almost broke you is building what you were called to become.
