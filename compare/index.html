Speakerfolio vs speaker directories and marketplaces

Most speaker directories charge a monthly plan, sell leads, or take a fee on bookings. Speakerfolio lists you free and gives you a full website, so planners who find you can book you straight away.

Marketplace and business software

Speakerfolio vs eSpeakers

A long-running speaker marketplace with calendaring and business tools. Basic costs $179 a year and Pro $85 a month.

Choose eSpeakers if you want a booking calendar and business reports in one paid tool.

Choose Speakerfolio if you want a free listing and a full speaker website built for you, without a subscription.

Free profiles, paid leads

Speakerfolio vs SpeakerHub

A free SpeakerPAGE profile, plus paid lead lists from $97 a month and a done-for-you outreach team from $1,000 a month. It says it takes no cut of your fees.

Choose SpeakerHub if you want large lists of event organisers to pitch.

Choose Speakerfolio if you want a real website, not only a profile, and planners who find you instead of you chasing them.

Paid job leads

Speakerfolio vs SpeakerMatch

Speaking job leads and applications, from $30 to $80 every four weeks or $325 to $845 a year. No free plan is listed.

Choose SpeakerMatch if you want to apply to posted speaking jobs.

Choose Speakerfolio if you want to be found and booked without paying every month.

Speaker and event matching

Speakerfolio vs SpeakUp

Organisers post speaker requests and speakers apply. Speakers can join free, with a Professional plan at $29 a month.

Choose SpeakUp if you want to apply to open speaker requests.

Choose Speakerfolio if you want a website and directory profile that win bookings on their own.

Marketplace for performers

Speakerfolio vs GigSalad

A marketplace for entertainers that also lists speakers. Paid memberships run $139 to $479, and GigSalad charges a 2.5% to 5% service fee on bookings made through the site.

Choose GigSalad if you also perform at parties and private events.

Choose Speakerfolio if you speak at conferences, companies and organisations and want to keep 100% of your fee.

Conference call-for-papers

Speakerfolio vs Sessionize

Many tech and community conferences take talk submissions through Sessionize. Events pay; speakers keep a profile they reuse for applications.

Use Sessionize to apply to conferences that run calls for papers.

Use Speakerfolio alongside it, so planners who look you up find a full website and can book you directly.

Speakerfolio vs speakers bureaus

BigSpeak, AAE Speakers Bureau, Executive Speakers Bureau, Premium Speakers Agency, Keppler Speakers and Washington Speakers Bureau work for event clients and represent a curated roster. They charge speakers nothing to list, but take a commission on each booking, typically 25% to 35% of the fee, and usually look for speakers with stage video, major-company testimonials and fees around $10,000 or more.

When a website builder makes sense

Choose Wix or Squarespace if you enjoy designing, want an online store or many custom pages, and have time to learn the editor. Wix's free plan shows Wix branding and ads on a wixsite.com address. Squarespace raised US prices in July 2026: Basic went from $16 to $19 a month on annual billing.

When Speakerfolio makes sense

Choose Speakerfolio if you want a finished speaker website without building it. We write the copy from your LinkedIn profile, add your talks, stage video and testimonials, set up a booking form, and list you in the directory. Read more: free vs paid speaker websites.

Speakerfolio vs speaker tools like Talkadot

Talkadot is an audience feedback tool: speakers add polls and questions to a talk to collect feedback and testimonials. It has a free Lite plan, with Pro at $49 and Elite at $99 a month. It does not list you or build your website, so it works alongside Speakerfolio: collect testimonials with a feedback tool, then show them on your Speakerfolio website and profile.

Questions

What is the best alternative to eSpeakers or SpeakerMatch?

If you want to be listed without a monthly plan, Speakerfolio is free: you get a profile in the speaker directory and a full speaker website, written and built for you, with no commission on bookings.

Is Speakerfolio a speakers bureau?

No. Bureaus such as BigSpeak, AAE, Keppler and Washington Speakers Bureau represent a selective roster and take a commission, typically 25% to 35% of the fee. Speakerfolio is a directory and website service: planners contact you directly and you keep your whole fee.

Can I use Speakerfolio and a speakers bureau at the same time?

Yes. Many speakers use a bureau for larger engagements and their own website and directory profile for direct bookings. Just keep your fees and details consistent.

How is Speakerfolio different from SpeakerHub?

SpeakerHub gives you a free profile and sells lead lists and outreach. Speakerfolio gives you a free directory profile plus a real one-page speaker website that we write and build for you.

Does Speakerfolio charge a booking fee like GigSalad?

No. GigSalad charges a 2.5% to 5% service fee on bookings made through its site. Speakerfolio takes no fee or commission; you agree the fee with the planner directly.

Is Speakerfolio cheaper than Wix or Squarespace for a speaker website?

Usually, yes. The free plan has no ads, and Premium on your own domain is $100 a year with the build and hosting included. Wix starts at $17 a month and Squarespace at $19 a month for your own domain, before your time to build the site.

Do these platforms share tips for speakers?

Many do. Speakerfolio publishes free guides on becoming a speaker, public speaking, speaker websites and getting booked, and also shares guides for event planners.
