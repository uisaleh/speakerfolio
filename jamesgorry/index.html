Meet James

A steel erector who knows the risk.

James (Jimmy) Gorry is a health and safety keynote and motivational speaker from Ireland. He spent nearly thirty years in steel fabrication and construction, starting as a welder in 1976 and running his own businesses in steel erection, demolition, crane hire and roofing. In 2005, on a routine roof repair in Dublin, he didn't clip on his harness. He fell 26 feet and was left paraplegic. Since 2008, through James Gorry Ltd, he has delivered talks to construction, industrial and manufacturing companies across Ireland, the UK and Europe, helping them build safety cultures where every worker returns home safely.

His story

From the steel to the stage.

1976

Welder and steel erector

Starts as a welder and fabricator at Butlers SIAC in Portarlington, then moves out to sites for repairs and steel erection.

1981

His own construction business

Starts ABS Construction in Monasterevin, Co. Kildare, working on site in steel erection, demolition, crane hire and roofing.

1989

Managing director

Runs Monasterevin Construction Services, a steel fabrication, building contracting and crane hire company.

2005

Three seconds, 26 feet

On a routine roof repair in Dublin he doesn't attach his harness. He slips, falls 26 feet and is left paraplegic. “I never classed it as a risk because I had done it so many times.”

2008

James Gorry Health & Safety

Starts speaking to companies about workplace safety, using his own accident to show what one shortcut can cost.

Today

Still on the road

Safety weeks, site inductions and conferences across Ireland, the UK and Europe, in person and online.
