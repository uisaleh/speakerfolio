About

Helping people rebuild after loss, grief and second chances.

Sadiq Zahir is an author, speaker and peer support advocate based in Norfolk, Virginia, and the founder of Sadiq Zahir Speaks. He speaks in schools, hospitals, reentry programs and community settings on rebuilding after incarceration, grief and the recovery of purpose.

He is the author of the memoir The Strength of the Surviving Twin (2026). Sadiq mentors young people with the Bridge The Gap Youth Program and leads a men’s accountability group in Hampton Roads. He is a Certified Life Skills Coach and CBT Certified Practitioner, with training in Motivational Interviewing.
