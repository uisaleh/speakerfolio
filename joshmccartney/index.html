About Josh

From performing on hotel stages to coaching leaders, I've spent my career learning what makes a room listen.

I started as a professional musician, with a music degree from Queensland University of Technology and a residency at Anantara Dubai Palm Jumeirah. I then ran Event Central, supplying entertainment to five-star hotels and corporate clients. In 2019 I founded THRIVE by Josh McCartney to help executives, individuals and teams communicate with confidence.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What does Josh speak about?

Communication and how it shapes the way people think and act, leadership communication, selling from the stage and memory techniques. He is a two-time TEDx speaker, at TEDxAlQuoz and TEDxDGPS Youth.

Does Josh train teams?

Yes. Through THRIVE by Josh McCartney he trains teams and leaders to present, pitch and communicate with confidence, at companies including Nokia, Adidas and Forbes Global Properties.

Can Josh host our event?

Yes. Josh works as an MC and host for corporate events, conferences and awards nights.

Does he coach individuals?

Yes. He coaches industry experts, founders and thought leaders to find their message, structure a talk, deliver it with confidence and get on the right stages.

Where is Josh based?

Josh is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For events elsewhere, add the location to your booking request.
